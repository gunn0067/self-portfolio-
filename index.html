Selected Engineering

Work

Problem

Stocks or crypto, which would have been better for a monthly SIP?

Approach

Backtested a ₹1,000/month SIP on five years of Indian market data (Nifty 50, Bitcoin); analysed the COVID-crash drawdown, India VIX (fear index) vs Nifty, and sector-wise recovery.

Findings

₹62,000 invested became ₹95,365 in Nifty and ₹4,43,239 in Bitcoin. COVID-crash drawdown: -45.6% vs -83.3%. Recovery from the bottom: IT +157%, FMCG +57%.

Limitation: a single five-year window; hindsight analysis.

Problem

Which factors are most associated with diabetes, heart disease, cancer and PCOS?

Approach

Loaded 7 public clinical datasets (not India-specific) into SQLite; SQL queries plus 13 visualisations.

Findings

Diabetes risk rises about 15x between the 20s and the 60s; hypertension and heart disease together are associated with about 6.4x higher risk. Observational data: associations, not causation.
